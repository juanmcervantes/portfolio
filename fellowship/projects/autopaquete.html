01 / FIELD NOTES

The challenge

Modern shipping operations span pricing, carriers, marketing, and customer experience. The opportunity is to make those moving parts easier to operate, measure, and improve.

02 / FIELD NOTES

My role

I lead the effort to build an AI-native operation while establishing the growth function from the ground up. My remit includes productivity methods, internal AI tools and agents, paid and organic acquisition experiments, and better connections between marketing and operational data.

03 / FIELD NOTES

What happened

Autopaquete describes a platform serving more than 5,000 users. The company’s expansion targets are ambitions, not results achieved by my work, so I do not present them as milestones here.

04 / FIELD NOTES

What I learned

Useful AI is operational: it should reduce friction, create leverage, and fit the habits of the people who use it. Experimentation becomes valuable when it is tied to reliable measurement.
