01 / FIELD NOTES

The challenge

For many early-career doctors, opening a private practice means committing to expensive leases before building a patient base. At the same time, medical offices stood underused during the pandemic.

02 / FIELD NOTES

My role

I co-founded Docstay with two partners and led product, user research, sales, marketing, and partnerships. We connected equipped offices with doctors who wanted flexible places to see patients, building a two-sided marketplace around trust and convenience.

03 / FIELD NOTES

What happened

Our platform listed more than 60 offices across Mexican cities, facilitated over 900 booking hours, and supported care for over 100 distinct patients. Some participating practitioners reported up to three times as many patients and higher income; that figure is a reported observation, not a controlled study.

04 / FIELD NOTES

What I learned

A useful marketplace is more than a directory. Trust, local supply, and genuine workflow fit matter more than shiny features. We eventually closed the business when the founding team’s circumstances changed.
