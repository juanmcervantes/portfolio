A CLOSER LOOK

The problem

Young doctors and independent healthcare practitioners faced expensive long-term leases and limited access to well-located offices. During the pandemic, many existing spaces were underused.

What I did

With two co-founders, I helped build Docstay, a marketplace for booking equipped medical offices. I led product management, sales, user research, partnerships, and marketing, while working with practitioners and office owners to build a service they could trust.

Outcomes & signals

  • 60+ equipped offices listed across Mexican cities
  • 900+ hours booked through the platform
  • 100+ distinct patients supported through listed offices
  • Participating practitioners reported up to 3× growth in patients and income

What I learned

The strongest value was not just booking space. Flexible access could let doctors serve patients in different parts of a city, lowering costs and increasing reach. The venture ultimately closed as the founding team's circumstances changed.
