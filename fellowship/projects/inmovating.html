01 / FIELD NOTES

The challenge

Buying a home in Sonora involved fragmented, offline steps. We saw an opening for a more digital journey—without fully appreciating how local buying habits shaped adoption.

02 / FIELD NOTES

My role

I co-founded Inmovating, led the technical team, contributed directly to a full-stack product, and coordinated integration work across the platform. We raised $25,000 from angel investors in Sonora and Arizona.

03 / FIELD NOTES

What happened

We shipped a technology platform and secured early financial backing, but scaling marketing faster than sales, insufficient user testing, and imperfect founder coordination held us back.

04 / FIELD NOTES

What I learned

Building software quickly is not the same as finding demand. Customer discovery, product iteration, and founder alignment have to keep pace with engineering.
