The challenge

Strong innovation ecosystems do not happen by accident. Founders, students, investors, and companies need opportunities to find one another and exchange practical knowledge.

02 / FIELD NOTES

My role

As co-founder and tech director of Jalisco Tech Week, I contribute to an event ecosystem that brings regional builders and industry leaders together through conversations, workshops, and connections.

The initiative creates a shared meeting point for Jalisco’s technology community. I have not included attendance numbers or economic impact figures because I cannot substantiate those here.
