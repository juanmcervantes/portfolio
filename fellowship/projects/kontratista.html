01 / FIELD NOTES

The challenge

Contractors manage complex teams, equipment, projects, and information. We wanted to understand what actually slowed them down before prescribing another platform.

02 / FIELD NOTES

My role

I co-founded Kontratista and visited construction sites to interview teams. We mapped the industry’s workflow problems, worked with ten design partners, and built an early MVP using familiar tools like Microsoft Teams and Asana.

03 / FIELD NOTES

What happened

Our discovery clarified the highest-friction workflows. We paused the venture strategically rather than forcing adoption before the market was ready.

04 / FIELD NOTES

What I learned

Do the unscalable thing first: visit the site, see the messy reality, and make the smallest useful prototype. Pausing can be a disciplined product decision.
