A CLOSER LOOK

The problem

Contractors navigate fragmented workflows and face adoption barriers for new digital tools, especially on active job sites.

What I did

Rather than designing solely from assumptions, we conducted site visits, interviews, and problem discovery with practitioners. We built a lightweight workflow MVP using tools familiar to teams, including Microsoft Teams and Asana.

Outcomes & signals

  • Worked with 10 design partners across business sizes
  • Built and tested a lightweight workflow MVP
  • Explored BIM practices and adoption challenges

What I learned

The team paused the venture when the market and adoption conditions did not support the intended trajectory. Direct observation and working with design partners proved more informative than a polished speculative product.
