02 / WHO I AM

Curious by default.
Practical by choice.

✳+

I have been building technology projects since 2018. I care about the future of Latin America—and about making ambitious tools genuinely useful to the people they are meant to serve.

I co-founded Docstay and Inmovating, explored the construction industry with Kontratista, and now work on AI-native operations and growth at Autopaquete. Beyond startups, I co-founded Jalisco Tech Week and founded Mariachis Valley to connect more local builders.

I am studying AI and Data Science at Tecnológico de Monterrey and have been part of the Global Shapers Community in Guadalajara since March 2024.
