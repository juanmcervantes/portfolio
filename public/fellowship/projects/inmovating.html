A CLOSER LOOK

The problem

Property purchase workflows remained fragmented and offline, leaving buyers and sellers with opaque, slow processes.

What I did

I led technical development and contributed hands-on to a full-stack real-estate platform. The team integrated third-party APIs, chatbot features, scraping, and remote engineering practices while exploring regional behaviors and market needs.

Outcomes & signals

  • Raised $25,000 USD from angel investors in Arizona and Sonora
  • Built a full-stack property-buying product
  • Coordinated a remote technical team

What I learned

We overinvested in product and marketing before enough direct user validation. Sales and operations lagged behind development, and communication among six co-founders was difficult. Those mistakes taught me to validate faster and influenced my next company, Docstay.
